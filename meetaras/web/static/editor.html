Good for a phone's own microphone. Turn it off when the sound is already cleaned up (e.g. the
        iPhone's Studio audio mix): the background stays as quiet as recorded. The loudness is set either way.

        demos); the export takes 3–4× as long and

How Meetaras works

      
    

    

      
Meetaras turns your talk recording into a YouTube-ready video. For every moment of the talk you
        choose what viewers see: your slides, you, or both. Those choices are the changes on the
        right, and they are saved as plain text in timeline.txt after every edit.
        The editor only edits that file; Export renders the actual video from it.


      
What viewers can see

      

        
        
        
        
        
      

      
A screen recording (for demos) works like a slide: C corner, Shift+C side by side, F full.
        Set where it starts with the offset field on the right.


      
Flow

      
    
        
  1. Start from the draft. Make video already found most slide changes by looking at the
          projector in your video. Lines marked CHECK are worth a look.
    2. 
        
  2. Trim. Go to where the talk really starts and press [; at the end press ].
    3. 
        
  3. Watch and fix. Play (Space), at 1.5–2× if you like. When a slide change is missing, press N
          (next slide) or click the slide in the strip below. Marks made while playing land 0.3 s earlier, to
          allow for reaction time.
    4. 
        
  4. Choose the style. Where you matter more than the slide, press P; where the corner covers
          content, Shift+S; for a dense slide, F. To give only part of a stretch another style, press
          K (Split here) where that part starts, then the style.
    5. 
        
  5. Leave out what you don't want. “Leave out” is a choice like the styles. Press K (Split here) where
          the unwanted part starts and again where it ends, click the piece in between on the bar, and press X
          (Leave out). It stays on the bar, hatched, and playback skips it. To bring it back, select it and choose a style.
    6. 
        
  6. Correct a change. Click it (on the coloured bar or in the list): the next key or slide click edits
          that change. Drag its left edge to move it, or type the time. Del (Unsplit) joins that part with the
          one before it; nothing is removed from the video. Ctrl+Z undoes.
    7. 
        
  7. Export preview to check the result quickly (720p), then Make video for the final video:
          it only re-renders what changed.
    8. 
      


      
Good to know

      
    
        
  • Everything saves automatically. Your original file is kept once as timeline.txt.orig.
    • 
        
  • You can also edit timeline.txt by hand; the editor notices and offers to reload.
    • 
        
  • With a separate microphone, this preview plays the camera sound; the export uses the microphone.
    • 
        
  • Your intro and outro are set in the header of timeline.txt (intro file.mp4,
          outro file.mp4). Export adds them; their sound is levelled separately.
          Preview them with the ▶ Intro / ▶ Outro buttons, or press Home to play everything as exported.
    • 
        
  • Every key also has a button in the bar above the timeline. Press ? to open this again; all keys are listed under “Keys” on the right.
